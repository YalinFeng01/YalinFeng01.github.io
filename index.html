👤 Biography

I am interested in generative models, visual AI, recommendation systems, and reliable experimental workflows. My work focuses on turning model behavior into measurable evidence, then shaping that evidence into usable research code and systems.

Recently, I have been exploring high-resolution visual generation, latent-space diagnostics, positional encoding behavior, and evaluation pipelines for controlled ablation studies. I also enjoy building practical tools around retrieval, text-to-SQL, and applied machine learning.

Many kind people helped me in my journey. If you want to talk about research, engineering, or collaboration, please feel free to reach out by email.

🫡 Motivation: "Why do I do research?"

  • To collaborate with people who think differently and build sharper ideas together.
  • For the joy of discovery: Insight | Innovation | Independence.
  • To make AI systems easier to understand, evaluate, and use in real workflows.
