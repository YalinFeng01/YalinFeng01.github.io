Yalin Feng

Learning visual concepts from less, then generating more.

Few-Shot Segmentation · Multimodal Learning · Generative Modeling

I am a final-year Master's student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). My research lies at the intersection of few-shot segmentation, multimodal learning, and generative modeling, with a focus on visual systems that can learn and adapt from limited supervision.

I explore how generative priors, vision-language representations, and foundation models can improve dense visual prediction—bridging perception and generation to build more adaptable, efficient, and reliable models.
