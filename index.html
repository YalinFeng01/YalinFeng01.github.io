👤 Biography

Welcome to my personal homepage!

I am currently a final-year Master's student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). My research interests lie firmly at the intersection of AIGC (AI-Generated Content), Multimodal Manga/Anime Generation, and Data-Centric AI. I am deeply passionate about pushing the boundaries of advanced generative modeling techniques, with a particular focus on Diffusion Models, Flow Matching, and MLLMs.

My primary research goal is to enhance visual consistency and production quality in digital art and animation using state-of-the-art AI. Currently, I am exploring how to optimize the SDXL VAE latent space using geometry-guided sparse correspondence and cross-view attention.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities or Research Assistant (RA) positions for the upcoming application cycle.

🫡 Motivation: "Why do I do research?"

  • To improve visual consistency and production quality in digital art and animation.
  • To build stronger generative modeling techniques around Diffusion Models, Flow Matching, and MLLMs.
  • To connect practical AI systems with creative production workflows.
