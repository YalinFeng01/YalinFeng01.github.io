Research direction

Models that see beyond the labels.

I study how visual models can generalize from limited supervision, and how generative priors and multimodal knowledge can make segmentation systems more adaptable, expressive, and reliable.

A

Few-Shot Segmentation

Semantic segmentation under limited annotations, foundation models, and prompt-guided adaptation.

B

Multimodal Learning

Vision-language understanding, cross-modal alignment, and transferable representations.

C

Generative Modeling

Diffusion, flow matching, latent generative models, and controllable generation.
